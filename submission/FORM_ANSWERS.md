# Directory submission answers

**Name:** Eqho
**Server URL:** https://mcp.eqho.ai/mcp
**Tagline:** Run your AI voice agents and calling campaigns from Claude.

**Description**
Eqho is a voice AI platform for businesses that live on the phone. Our AI agents make and take real calls, follow up by text, and log every conversation with a transcript, recording, and outcome. This connector puts the whole platform inside Claude.

Ask Claude how last week's campaign performed and it pulls the dashboard. Ask why a call went sideways and it reads the transcript and the QA scorecard. Need a new agent for a reactivation campaign? Claude can draft the role, pick a voice, set the call window, and stage the campaign for you to review before anything goes live.

Anything that costs money or touches a real person, like placing a call, sending an SMS, starting a campaign, uploading leads, or buying a phone number, always asks for your approval first. If you manage multiple client accounts, you can switch between organizations in the same conversation.

**Use cases**
Agencies and operators use Eqho in Claude to build and tune AI calling agents, launch and monitor outbound campaigns, and dig into call results without leaving the conversation. The most common workflows are performance reporting, transcript and disposition review, and standing up new campaigns end to end.

**Example prompts**
1. Show me connect rate, appointments booked, and top dispositions for my solar campaign over the last 7 days.
2. Find calls this week where the lead mentioned a competitor and summarize what they said.
3. Create a new agent for our insurance renewal campaign using a warm female voice, then run the campaign checklist and tell me what's missing before launch.
4. Switch to the Acme Roofing org and tell me which campaigns are live right now.

**Auth:** OAuth 2.0 with PKCE (API key fallback). Sessions scoped to the user's Eqho org permissions.
